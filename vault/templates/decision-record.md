# Decision

State the decision in one sentence, in the imperative.

## Context
What was true that made this a question at all.

## Decision Made
What was chosen.

## Why
The reasoning. Include the evidence, and include the counter-argument you rejected --
a decision record without the losing case is a press release.

## Alternatives Considered
1. **Option.** Rejected because...

## Consequences
What this costs, what it forecloses, what has to change.

## Review Date
A real date. Put it in your calendar or it is decoration.
This field is the most commonly unused one in any decision template; using it is what
separates a decision log from an archive.
